MTech · Hazard analysis

Electrical HAZOP study

A review of electrical hazards across eight distribution nodes in an R&D and QA facility using IEC 61882 methodology.

Context

MTech micro project · November 2025

Scope

I examined the main distribution board, sub-boards, lab sockets, UPS interface, and earthing network as part of an eight-node study.

Method

I applied HAZOP guidewords such as No, More, Less, and Reverse to identify deviations, causes, and consequences. I used a 4 × 4 severity and likelihood matrix to rank the findings.

Findings and recommendations

The study flagged inadequate earthing resistance, missing surge protection, and untested residual current circuit breakers. Recommendations included portable appliance testing, equipotential bonding, and surge protection devices.
