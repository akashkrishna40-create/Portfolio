MTech · Occupational ergonomics

Prolonged standing in textile retail

An observation and checklist-based study of prolonged standing among textile shop workers.

Context

MTech micro project

Scope

I studied the ergonomic risks for textile retail workers who spend much of their shift standing. The project considered shifts involving six hours of standing and, in some cases, more than ten.

Method

I used direct observation and a structured occupational health checklist to examine standing duration, break frequency, and reported symptoms such as leg pain, lower back pain, and swelling.

Recommendations

I proposed anti-fatigue mats, access to seating, and job rotation to help reduce prolonged standing exposure.
