Overview & Commissioning Methodology

During my tenure as Trainee Electrical Engineer at Sud Chemie India Pvt Ltd, I actively supported the electrical development, panel installation, field cable routing, pre-commissioning testing, and final operational handover of industrial plant equipment.

All installation work was executed under strict permit-to-work (PTW) protocols, job hazard briefings, and Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) energy isolation procedures to guarantee safety across production lines.

Commissioning Handover & Safety Verification

Before handing over newly installed apparatus to production operations, systematic pre-commissioning checks were conducted:

  • Insulation resistance (Megger) testing and phase-to-phase continuity verification on feeder cables and motor windings.
  • Uncoupled motor rotational direction checks and control circuit dry-run testing.
  • Verification of thermal overload relay (OLR) settings against motor full-load current (FLC) ratings.
  • Full functional testing of emergency stop (E-stop) loops, limit switches, and process interlocks.
  • Single-line diagram (SLD) updates in AutoCAD and CADMate for permanent engineering records.
